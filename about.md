> **Mod Description**

**Rover Science Continued - Kerbalism Edition** is a KSP mod/patch that provides a way for `Rover Science Continued` (mod adopted by LinuxGuruGamer) and `Kerbalism` to work well together. For compatibility, a small part of RSC is suppressed and RSCKE will make sure to store/track its Science Experiments with Kerbalism API, instead of Stock's. But it goes a little beyond. With **RSCKE** installed, players can still enjoy the complete RSC gameplay loop, while benefiting from an enhanced UI and also taking advantage of Kerbalism "natural" rebalancing - which, for example, prevents unlimited amounts of Science from being gathered with the same experiment by design.
